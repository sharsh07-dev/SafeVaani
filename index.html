Your questions, answered

SafeVaani (also written SafeVaani Safety) is a women's personal-safety app for India built around hands-free Voice SOS. In an emergency you simply say a word out loud, "help", "bachao", or "madad", and SafeVaani alerts your trusted circle with your live location, even with the phone locked and even offline. The word is recognised on your own device, so listening audio is never uploaded, and your location is never sold. It is live on Google Play and free to start.

Yes. The core safety features, Voice SOS, alerts to your trusted circle, and one-tap access to 112, are free for every woman. SafeVaani Plus (₹149/month) adds the everyday layer, such as live location on the map, safe zones and video during an SOS, and SafeVaani Family (₹499/month) covers up to four people you love.

Yes. Voice detection works offline. With no data, your SOS goes out as a text message with your location, and with no signal at all it can be relayed through a nearby phone running SafeVaani, which needs another SafeVaani phone in range.

No. Voice detection runs on your phone to recognise your trigger word, and that listening audio is never uploaded, streamed, or stored on a server. Audio leaves your phone in only three cases, and each one is something you did: when an SOS fires, its recording is saved to your private history so what happened exists afterwards; when you start an Audio Escort, your circle hears short live clips that are deleted about 24 hours later; and if you volunteer for Voice Quests, you record a distress word yourself to help train our Indian-accent model. Voice Quests are off by default and you can withdraw whenever you like.

Only the people you choose. Your location goes to your circle during an active SOS, during a Safe Journey, during an Audio Escort you started, and while you have Circle sharing switched on. Circle sharing is off until you turn it on yourself, everyone in the circle can see who is currently visible, and your trail is deleted after 7 days. Nobody outside your circle ever sees it, and SafeVaani never sells location data to anyone.

SafeVaani runs on Android 8.0 and above. An iPhone (iOS) version is planned but not available yet.

Yes. "SafeVaani" and "SafeVaani Safety" are the same product, a hands-free voice-SOS women's safety app for India. Some people also type it as "SafeVaani" or "the SafeVaani app"; it all refers to the same app made by SafeVaani Safety.

SafeVaani listens on your device for a distress word like "help". The moment it hears it, a short countdown starts, and then your trusted circle is alerted with your live location and recording begins, all without you touching your phone.

Not while SafeVaani is listening. Detection happens entirely on-device, so there is nothing there for us to leak or sell. The audio SafeVaani does hold is the recording from an SOS you raised, saved to your private history; the clips of an Audio Escort you started, heard only by that circle and deleted about 24 hours after they were recorded; and any Voice Quest clip you chose to send. None of it is ever sold.

Audio Escort lets the people in one of your circles listen live to what is around you, for example while you walk home late. You start it yourself, or accept when someone in your circle asks. You choose how long it runs, up to three hours, set a stop code only you know, and can end it any time. Your phone shows a notification the whole time it is on, your circle sees your exact location until it ends, and every clip is deleted about 24 hours after it was recorded.

Every SOS begins with a countdown you can cancel with a single tap, so a false alarm never reaches anyone. Accidental triggers are forgiven; real ones alert everyone the moment the countdown ends.
